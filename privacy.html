This policy describes Nova Calc and this product website.

In the app

Nova Calc does not require a separate account. Calculation history and notes are stored on your device. The app uses Apple's iCloud services to sync history through your personal iCloud account when available. We do not operate a server that receives your calculations.

The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store and app-usage information according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policies.

Managing your records

You can manage and delete calculation records inside the app. Device and iCloud settings are managed through Apple's system settings.

On this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

When you contact us

GitHub support issues are public. Please do not post sensitive calculations, contact details or other private information. Information you choose to include is used to respond to your request.

Questions

Visit our support page for help or to report a concern.
